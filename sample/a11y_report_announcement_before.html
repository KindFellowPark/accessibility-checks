What this report does not decide

The checks answer questions with one correct answer. Judgment calls stay with a person, and naming them is part of the report rather than a footnote to it.

  • Whether alt text is accurateThe check proves an alt attribute exists. Whether it describes the image is a human read.
  • Whether alt="" is appropriateAn empty alt correctly hides a decorative image. Deciding an image is decorative is a human call.
  • Text styled to look like a headingBold text standing in for a heading is caught in Word files, not on web pages. On a page, a person checks that every visual heading is a real heading tag.
  • Reading orderThe checks look at heading levels, not whether the page's sequence makes sense read aloud.
  • Text over photos or gradientsContrast over a non-solid background cannot be resolved from the source. Such a pairing is reported as unresolved and fails, rather than passing silently.
  • Anything only a live page showsFocus traps in a pop-up, motion, and screen-reader announcement order need a rendered page and assistive technology, not a source scan.
